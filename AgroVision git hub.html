About AgroVision

Better decisions before the first seed

AgroVision is a college project that shows how a simple web tool can help farmers plan with confidence.

Choosing what to grow depends on soil, water and season. AgroVision brings these together on one screen, so a farmer can see the weather, compare crops against local conditions and read practical advice without searching several places.

This version runs entirely in your browser with sample data. A full version could connect to live weather services and local soil records.

  • See conditions earlyTemperature, humidity and rainfall help you time sowing and irrigation.
  • Match crop to landThe recommender scores each crop against your soil and rainfall.
  • Learn as you goCrop cards and tips explain seasons, water needs and common problems.
